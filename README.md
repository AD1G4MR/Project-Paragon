Project Paragon is a hero shooter set in a world threatened by monstrous forces, where powerful individuals known as Paragons are recruited from different worlds to defend humanity. The project explores character-driven gameplay through three unique heroes: Dokk-3n, a heavily armed engineer and soldier; The Revenant, a bounty hunter who utilises voodoo-based abilities; and The Time Wraith, a futuristic scientist who manipulates advanced technology. Each character was designed with their own identity, abilities and playstyle - encouraging players to approach combat in different ways.

Alongside combat systems and character abilities, the project also incorporates environmental storytelling through a narrative showcase level that introduces the world and its key characters. Through the development of Project Paragon, I strengthened my Unreal Engine 5 skills, particularly in gameplay programming, ability systems, character design, balancing and narrative-driven game development.
